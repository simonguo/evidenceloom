# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Evidence Loom a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technical choices.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and focus on what is best for the community.
- Keep financial discussions educational; do not pressure others to trade or disclose private positions.
- Never publish another person's credentials, private reports, or identifying information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, trolling, sustained disruption, and sexualized attention are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
